# Dataset changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-24)
- Added crop_water_mm and labor_days_per_acre (A1) for the digital twin's weekly water and labour views. Planning values unchanged.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)
- First demonstration release for Buldhana district, Maharashtra: 7 kharif and 6 rabi crops enabled, 2 records excluded with reasons.
- All numeric values are synthetic prototype assumptions (code A1). Kharif compatibility pairs are A2 (reported by an earlier prototype, unverified here).
