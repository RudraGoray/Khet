How it works

Don't Just Plan Your Farm. See It Come Alive.

  1. 01

    Tell Us About Your Farm

    Enter or speak your plot size, season, water availability, and preferences. Hindi, Marathi and English all work, and you check every field before anything is planned.

  2. 02

    Build Your Crop Plan

    Get a crop allocation, planting calendar, and explanation of the choices. Every plan is checked against the rules for area, season, crop pairs and water by a separate validator.

  3. 03

    Watch Your Season Unfold

    Visualize crop growth, water use, harvests, and financial outcomes on your digital twin, week by week, from sowing to the last harvest.

      Features

      Designed Around Real Farming Decisions.

      खेत शेत Farm
      01 · Voice input

      Speak in Hindi, Marathi or English.

      Press Speak and describe your farm the way you would to a neighbour. KhetPlan reads the district, plot size, season, water and risk, shows you exactly what it heard, and waits for your confirmation. Typing works too.

      02 · Crop planning  ·  03 · 3D growth

      A plan you can watch grow.

      An optimiser (OR-Tools CP-SAT) splits the plot into 20 strips and picks two or three compatible crops for your season and water. The plan is planted on a to-scale 3D field that grows, flowers and is harvested week by week, with sales, water and labour alongside.

      • Allocation, calendar and plain-language reasons
      • Every plan re-checked by an independent validator
      • Play, pause, scrub and orbit the season
      04 · What-if experiments

      Break the season on purpose.

      Dry spells, pests, price changes and water cuts are replayed on your own plan. When the water runs short, KhetPlan can re-plan for what is left.

      Mixed crops vs. one crop, 1,000 seasons.

      Illustrative simulation with synthetic data, not a forecast.

      06 · Farm reports

      One printable page.

      Download a one-page A4 summary in English, हिन्दी or मराठी, plus the plan as data.
